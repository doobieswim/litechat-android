## What NOBODY has (our real opportunities)

### 1. Honest phone check (we have this) 🟢

The compatibility matrix that says "your phone can't run this" is the most honest thing in mobile AI. Nobody else does it. Everyone says "works on all devices." We say the truth.

**This is our brand.** Trust and honesty.

### 2. Slash commands (we have this) 🟢

`/imagine` `/browse` `/video` — feels like a terminal, not a toy. No competitor does slash commands on mobile. Everyone uses menus and buttons.

**This makes us the "power user" app.** Fast, keyboard-driven, no fat.

### 3. RAM-pressure awareness (partially built) 🟡

The app automatically downgrades when RAM is low. Smaller image cache. Lower quality images. Tougher streaming throttle. Nobody does adaptive performance — they just crash on weak phones.

**This makes us the "it actually works" app.** 

### 4. Conversation as files 🆕

Every chat lives as a standalone file in a folder. You can:
- Share it like a document
- Back it up by copying a file
- Email a conversation
- Open old chats without "importing" anything

Most apps lock your chats inside a database. We treat chats like documents. This is the Notepad philosophy applied to AI chat.

### 5. Real-time cost display 🆕

After every AI response, show a tiny line: "This message cost $0.0003." Users KNOW they're not being ripped off. Builds insane trust. Easy to build — just count tokens.

**Nobody shows this. It's radical transparency.**

### 6. Community prompts 🆕

A simple list of prompts shared by other LiteChat users. No accounts. No login. Just a JSON file hosted on GitHub that the app fetches. Users contribute via PR. 

**Creates a community around the app without building a platform.**

### 7. LAN auto-detect 🆕

If you have Ollama running on a PC on your home wifi, the app auto-detects it and offers to switch. No configuration needed. Works offline with your own model.

**The "works without internet" angle is huge in developing countries.**

### 8. Screenshot-to-ask 🆕

Take a screenshot, switch to LiteChat, it detects the new screenshot and offers "Ask about this?" One tap. Faster than any attachment flow in any competitor.
