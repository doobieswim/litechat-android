## The human said this. It is law.

- The **vibe and the architecture** show a small app going against the fat, expensive, normal ones.
- It is **for regular people**.
- We **do not tell** them it is a fight.
- We **do not** use fight / wrestling / “underdog champ” talk in anything a user reads.
- It still has to feel like a **normal chat app** someone would open on a Tuesday.

One sentence: **Be the used Honda that always starts. Never be the poster that yells “UNDERDOG.”**

---

## Show, don’t tell

People believe what the app *does*. They bounce when the app *explains its personality*.

| They should feel | We never say | We do instead |
|---|---|---|
| This one is small | “tiny warrior / underdog / still standing” | Install is fast. Icon is simple. APK stays small. |
| This one is honest | “we tell the truth unlike the others” | If the phone is tight, a plain note says so. No speech. |
| This one is mine | “bring your own brain / we never see your key” | Key stays on the phone. No account wall. |
| This one doesn’t charge rent | “the champ rents the ring” | Free with one small banner, or pay once. |
| This one is for me | “for the people” | Words a non-dev can read. Big tap targets. One job: type, get an answer. |

If a sentence would make sense on a boxing poster, delete it.

## Banned in anything a user sees

Play listing, screenshots, onboarding, Settings, Pro screen, README-for-users, privacy page, notifications.

Do not use:

- fighter, underdog, champ, title, bout, ring, weigh-in, kick out, finisher, unmasked, fighting spirit, rope-a-dope, King’s Road, ōdō, prize fight, vs the giants
- “they laughed,” “still up,” “50-megabyte splash”
- naming Agora / ChatGPT as enemies on the store page
- “power user,” “vim of AI,” “for developers only”

Internal docs and agent notes may keep the metaphor. **Users never see it.**

## Allowed voice (everyday)

Short. Calm. Useful. Like a good hardware store, not a startup, not a gym.

Play subtitle shape (examples, not final):

- “Chat with AI using your own key. No monthly bill.”
- “Your key. Your phone. No account.”
- “AI chat that stays small.”

Onboarding first screen:

> Paste your API key (or pick a provider).  
> Then just chat.  
> If this phone is low on memory, we’ll say so.

Pro screen:

> Free includes a small banner.  
> Pay once to remove it. No monthly charge.

Error when the phone is too tight:

> This phone is low on memory. Chat still works. Heavy extras are off.

That last line *is* the underdog. Nobody needs the word.

## Architecture that *is* the vibe

This is where the fight lives. Users should never have to be told.

1. **Small first.** APK stays thin. No WebView chat shell. No bundled brain. No “agent lab” as the default.
2. **Opens on a cheap phone.** First message is the product. If it janks, we failed the theme.
3. **Honest gate, plain words.** Compat matrix can stay. Labels sound like a phone setting, not a manifesto. Green = fine. Yellow = tight. Red = this extra is off.
4. **No landlord.** No forced account. Key on device. Their API bill is theirs. If we ever show cost, it is a quiet receipt, not a flex.
5. **One small ask.** One banner, or $4.99 once. Never a surprise club.
6. **Refuse fat work.** If something would melt a 4GB phone, it is off or behind a clear “this is heavy” — not marketed as magic.
7. **Same app next year.** Don’t grow a new personality every update. Make the same small app *more reliable*. That is the long story, with no story told.

Coding agents: if a feature makes us louder, fatter, or more “special,” it fails this theme even if the ticket is Ready.

## Who it is for (everyday, not a club)

A person who:

- has a normal or old Android
- maybe already pays OpenAI / Groq / a cheap key, or will paste one
- is tired of $20/month *or* tired of apps that crawl
- does not care about “BYOK” as a word
- just wants to type and get an answer

They should not need a Reddit thread to understand the app.

Devs and 4GB nerds will *notice* the bones. That is enough. We do not make a costume for them either.

## Name / listing test (use this before any rebrand)

Read the name + 80-character subtitle out loud to someone who does not build apps.

Fail if they say: “is this a game?” “is this for hackers?” “why are they angry?”  
Pass if they say: “oh, I put my own key in and chat.”
